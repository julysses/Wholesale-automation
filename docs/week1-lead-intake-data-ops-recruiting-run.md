## 1) Role Brief (Published)

### Role mission
Own inbound lead intake quality and daily data operations so acquisition reps only work clean, correctly routed, high-priority opportunities.

### Scope
- Validate inbound records before CRM import (field completeness, duplicate checks, phone/email normalization).
- Run daily lead import ops from forms, CSV drops, and webhook feeds.
- Route leads into HOT/WARM/COLD queues based on policy and SLA windows.
- Monitor failed imports and webhook errors; resolve or escalate inside SLA.
- Maintain auditable QA logs for imported/updated records.

### Success metrics (first 30 days)
- Data QA pass rate: >= 98%.
- Duplicate leakage into active queue: <= 1.5%.
- Import-to-queue routing latency: <= 15 minutes median.
- Failed import resolution: <= 4 hours for P1 errors.
- HOT lead routing compliance: >= 99% within SLA.

### Required profile
- 2+ years in RevOps, Sales Ops, CRM operations, or lead operations.
- Strong spreadsheet/CSV hygiene and QA mindset.
- Experience with CRM stage management and operational SOP discipline.
- Able to troubleshoot API/webhook failures from logs and retries.
