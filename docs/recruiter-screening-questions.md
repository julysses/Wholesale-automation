# Recruiter Screening Guides (Week 2 Roles)

These questions are designed for a 20-minute initial screen to filter for technical aptitude, process discipline, and cultural fit.

---

## 1. AI Dialer Operations Specialist

**Goal**: Filter for candidates who can manage complex technical stacks and maintain 99%+ uptime.

### Technical & Tooling
1. "Which AI voice agents or automated dialers have you managed at scale? (Retell AI, Vapi, Air AI, etc.)"
   - *Look for*: Specificity on platform features, limitations, and JSON/webhook experience.
2. "Walk me through your process for preparing a lead list in BatchData before it hits the dialer."
   - *Look for*: Mention of deduplication, skip-tracing quality checks, and DNC scrubbing.
3. "If you see a '401 Unauthorized' error on your webhook logs during a live campaign, what is your immediate first step?"
   - *Look for*: Checking API keys/tokens, environment variables, and immediate communication to the manager.

### Process & Reliability
4. "This role requires 8 AM DFW (Dallas-Fort Worth) start times. What is your setup for internet and power redundancy?"
   - *Look for*: Backup ISP, UPS, or secondary work location.
5. "Describe a time you caught a data error *after* a campaign started. How did you handle the recovery?"

### Compensation & Fit
6. "Our target base for this role is [Range] plus performance bonuses tied to webhook completeness and failed job rates. Does this align with your expectations?"
7. "Why real estate wholesaling? What interests you about the 'distressed property' niche?"

## 2. Acquisitions Closer

**Goal**: Filter for high-empathy, high-urgency closers who follow the "motivation-first" strategy.

### Sales Style & Strategy
1. "Walk me through a recent negotiation where the seller's asking price was significantly higher than your Maximum Allowable Offer (MAO)."
   - *Look for*: Anchoring techniques, focus on pain points rather than price, and professional "walk-away" discipline.
2. "How do you handle the 'I need to think about it' objection when you know the property is facing foreclosure in 10 days?"
   - *Look for*: Urgency creation without being "pushy," empathy, and focusing on the consequence of inaction.
3. "What is your process for distinguishing a 'tire kicker' from a truly motivated seller?"
   - *Look for*: Timeline questions, condition of property, and hardship discovery.

### Speed & Hygiene
4. "We have a strict 60-minute follow-up SLA for HOT leads. How do you organize your day to ensure you never miss that window?"
   - *Look for*: CRM notification discipline and time-blocking for follow-ups.
5. "How much time do you spend on CRM notes per lead? Why is that detail important?"
   - *Look for*: Understanding of handoffs to Dispo/TC and data continuity.

### Compensation & Fit
6. "This is a base + commission role with [Range] per closed deal. Does this structure motivate you?"
7. "What is your target 'contracts per month' goal for yourself?"
8. "Are you comfortable with a role-play exercise in the next round?"

## 3. Disposition Coordinator

**Goal**: Filter for high-velocity, detail-oriented Dispo Coordinators who can match deals and manage bidding wars.

### Buyer Network & Strategy
1. "Which buyer segments (Fix & Flip, Buy & Hold, Institutional) have you managed or sold to at scale? (How many active buyers on your list?)"
   - *Look for*: Specificity on list size, segmentation by buy box, and repeat-buyer percentage.
2. "Walk me through your process for activating a buyer list once a new deal is under contract. (What is your 'blast' vs. 'direct reach-out' strategy?)"
   - *Look for*: Urgency (sub-2-hour outreach), deal packet quality, and multi-channel communication (SMS, Email, Calls).
3. "How do you handle a 'bidding war' between two high-intent buyers? (Walk me through your process for securing 'highest and best' offers.)"
   - *Look for*: Structured deadlines, transparent but firm communication, and margin protection discipline.

### Process & Detail
4. "What are the three most critical pieces of information you include in a 'Deal Packet' for a buyer? Why?"
   - *Look for*: Photos/Video, Rehab Estimate, Comps/ARV, and clear "Why it's a deal" logic.
5. "If a buyer backs out 48 hours before closing, what is your immediate recovery plan to prevent a deal collapse?"
   - *Look for*: Back-up buyer outreach, re-marketing urgency, and coordination with TC.

### Compensation & Fit
6. "Our target compensation is base + performance bonuses tied to assignment fees and dispo velocity. Does this align with your expectations?"
7. "What is your target 'days-to-dispo' goal for a standard distressed deal?"
8. "Are you comfortable with a practical exercise involving a disposition plan for a sample deal?"

## 4. Transaction Coordinator (Part-Time)

**Goal**: Filter for detail-oriented transaction coordinators who can manage the administrative and compliance lifecycle of a deal.

### Process & Detail Orientation
1. "Walk me through your checklist for a new contract-to-close file. What are the first 3 things you check or verify?"
   - *Look for*: Earnest Money Deposit status, Inspection period deadlines, and Title commitment request.
2. "How do you manage multiple files with overlapping deadlines? What tools or systems do you use to stay organized?"
   - *Look for*: Task management tools, calendar alerts, and systematic daily status checks.
3. "Describe a time when you identified a title or escrow issue that could have derailed a closing. How did you resolve it?"
   - *Look for*: Problem-solving mindset, proactive communication with stakeholders, and persistence.

### Communication & Collaboration
4. "How do you handle a situation where an agent or buyer is consistently late with paperwork or signatures?"
   - *Look for*: Professional persistence, escalating when necessary, and clear communication of consequences (e.g., closing delay).
5. "What is your process for keeping all parties (buyers, sellers, title, internal teams) updated on the status of a transaction?"
   - *Look for*: Regular status reports, clear and concise communication, and "closing-at-a-glance" visibility.

### Compensation & Fit
6. "Our target compensation is [Range] for this part-time role. Does this align with your expectations?"
7. "What is your typical 'file-to-close' success rate?"
8. "Are you comfortable with a practical exercise involving a transaction file review?"
