## 1) Role Brief (Published)

### Role mission
Secure a high-velocity Disposition Coordinator to own the end-to-end sales process for our property inventory. This role turns contracts into revenue by matching properties with our cash buyer network, managing bidding wars, and ensuring maximum assignment margins.

### Scope
- **Buyer Activation**: First buyer outreach within 2 hours of contract-ready package hitting the market.
- **Deal Marketing**: Prepare and distribute "Deal Packets" (photos, rehab estimates, comps, and property details) to target buyer segments.
- **Bid Management**: Conduct structured bidding processes to ensure we achieve target assignment fees and margin protection.
- **Pipeline Velocity**: Maintain a "Dispo Velocity" of less than 10 days from market-ready to signed assignment contract.
- **Buyer Intelligence**: Manage and grow our cash buyer list, tracking preferences (location, asset type, buy box) to ensure high-quality matching.

### Success metrics (first 30 days)
- **Buyer Activation Speed**: 100% of deals marketed to buyers within 2 hours of package readiness.
- **Dispo Velocity**: Median days from market-ready to assignment < 10 days.
- **Margin Protection**: 95%+ of deals closed at or above target assignment fee.
- **Buyer Relationship Quality**: Repeat-buyer participation rate > 60%.

### Required profile
- 2+ years in real estate dispositions, wholesale property sales, or investor relations.
- Proven track record of managing a high-volume buyer list and closing 10+ assignments per month.
- Elite communication skills with the ability to build trust and drive urgency in negotiations.
- High-level detail orientation and CRM hygiene (experience with GoHighLevel, Podio, or similar).
- Experience with distressed property analysis and "buy box" segmentation.

## 3) Practical Exercise: Disposition Plan for Sample Deal Packet

### Scenario
The candidate is given a "Deal Packet" for a distressed property in DFW (e.g., 123 Main St).
- **Purchase Price (under contract)**: $150,000.
- **Target Assignment Fee**: $15,000.
- **Target Sales Price**: $165,000.
- **Estimated ARV**: $250,000.
- **Rehab Estimate**: $40,000.
- **Comps**: 3 similar sold properties in the $240k-$260k range.

### Tasks
1. **List Segmentation**: Identify which buyer segments (Fix & Flip, Buy & Hold, Institutional) this property fits best.
2. **Blast Strategy**: Draft the initial SMS/Email blast to the buyer list.
3. **Urgency Tactics**: Propose a strategy for managing multiple offers (e.g., "highest and best" by Friday at 5 PM).
4. **Handoff Plan**: Define what information is needed from the buyer to hand off to the Transaction Coordinator for a smooth close.
5. **Negotiation & Objection Handling**: Role-play/Response to a low-ball offer ($155k) from a VIP buyer who claims "the rehab is actually $60k, not $40k" while you have a $165k bid from a new buyer. Demonstrate margin protection and relationship management.

## 6) Final Interview: Margin Discipline Scenario Set

**Purpose:** CEO-requested. Explicitly stress-test margin protection under VIP buyer pressure — the failure point that eliminated Elena Rossi. These 3 questions must be embedded in every final interview, not treated as optional.

**Format:** Role-play / verbal response. Interviewer plays the VIP buyer. No scripts given to candidate in advance.

---

### Scenario Q1 — The VIP Low-Ball with a Rehab Dispute

> "You have a deal under contract for $165k target assignment fee. Your top VIP buyer — someone who has closed 8 deals with you this year — comes back at $155k and says their contractor estimates $60k in rehab, not the $40k in your packet. You also have a new buyer at $163k who has never transacted with you. What do you do?"

**What we're listening for:**
- Does not immediately capitulate on price
- Acknowledges VIP relationship without letting it compromise the margin
- Uses the competing bid as leverage without burning the VIP (e.g., "I have another offer at $163k — I can hold the deal for you until [specific deadline] if you want to close at $163k or better")
- Proposes a path to verify the rehab discrepancy (third-party contractor call) rather than accepting the VIP's framing
- Does NOT offer to split the difference unprompted

**Red flag answer:** Any version of "let me see what I can do" without anchoring back to the target price.

---

### Scenario Q2 — The Disappearing Offer

> "You've run a 'highest and best' process. Your winner verbally committed at $167k. 48 hours later they go silent. Your second offer was $161k and has expired. The contract deadline is in 5 days. What's your move?"

**What we're listening for:**
- Re-engages winner with a firm deadline (e.g., "I need a signed assignment contract by [date] or I release the deal")
- Simultaneously re-activates the $161k buyer and 2–3 backup buyers without disclosing the winner's price
- Does not discount preemptively to re-engage — holds position until deadline forces a decision
- Has a clear escalation path: re-blast to buyer list if both expire

**Red flag answer:** Drops price proactively or waits passively for the winner to resurface.

---

### Scenario Q3 — The Institutional Squeeze

> "An institutional buyer (iBuyer/fund) offers $162k but requires a 30-day inspection period and a $10k earnest money deposit with soft terms. Your target fee is $15k. A smaller Fix & Flip buyer offers $158k with a 7-day close and hard EMD of $5k. How do you evaluate and what do you recommend to the team?"

**What we're listening for:**
- Correctly identifies that the institutional offer's soft terms and 30-day window create velocity risk (our KPI: < 10 days)
- Quantifies the risk: a blown institutional deal kills velocity metrics and holds the property off market for 30+ days
- Recommends the Fix & Flip buyer at $158k if the assignment fee still meets floor (asks what the floor is before recommending)
- Does not default to "bigger buyer = better" without analyzing terms

**Red flag answer:** Recommends the higher dollar offer without accounting for close certainty and dispo velocity impact.

---

### Scoring Guide (per question)

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pass (3/3)** | Holds margin, uses competing leverage, proposes a specific action with a deadline |
| **Conditional (2/3)** | Holds position but misses the leverage play or doesn't offer a re-engagement path |
| **Fail (1/3 or less)** | Concedes on price unprompted or gives a vague "I'll figure it out" response |

A candidate must score **Pass or Conditional on at least 2 of 3** to receive an offer recommendation.
