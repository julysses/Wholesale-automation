## 1) Role Brief (Published)

### Role mission
Convert HOT leads into signed acquisition contracts by providing immediate follow-up, expert underwriting, and professional negotiation.

### Scope
- **HOT Lead Intervention**: First human touch within 60 minutes of AI lead qualification.
- **Underwriting**: Verify property details, assess distress level, and calculate MAO (Maximum Allowable Offer) based on local comps.
- **Negotiation**: Conduct high-stakes negotiations with distressed sellers to secure favorable terms.
- **Appointment Conversion**: Move qualified leads to formal property inspections or virtual walkthroughs.
- **CRM Hygiene**: Maintain real-time updates in the CRM for pipeline visibility.

### Success metrics (first 30 days)
- **Speed-to-contact**: 100% of HOT leads contacted within 60 minutes.
- **Appointment Conversion**: >= 25% of HOT leads converted to appointments.
- **Offer Discipline**: 100% of offers within approved MAO guardrails.
- **CRM Integrity**: Daily stage updates and next-step notes completed for all active leads.

### Required profile
- 3+ years in high-volume sales (Real Estate Wholesaling preferred).
- Proven track record of closing 5+ deals per month.
- Expert-level negotiation skills and objection handling.
- Proficiency with CRM systems (Podio, GoHighLevel, or similar).
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment.

## 3) Practical Exercise: Distressed Seller Role-Play

### Objective
Assess the candidate's ability to build rapport, uncover motivation, and handle pricing objections with a distressed seller.

### Scenario
The candidate is following up on a HOT lead: a seller (Mrs. Johnson) who inherited a property in DFW that needs $40k in repairs. She wants $250k. Our MAO is $195k.

### Role-Play Guidelines
- **Seller Motivation**: Inherited property, living out of state, needs cash for personal reasons.
- **Objection 1 (Price)**: "Zillow says it's worth $300k."
- **Objection 2 (Timeline)**: "I need to close in 14 days."
- **Objection 3 (Process)**: "I don't want a bunch of people walking through my house."

### Evaluation Criteria
- **Empathy & Rapport**: Does the candidate listen and acknowledge the seller's situation?
- **Motivation Discovery**: Do they ask "why" she wants to sell now?
- **Pricing Discipline**: Do they stick to the MAO or explain the "why" behind our offer (repairs, holding costs)?
- **Closing Skill**: Do they move the conversation toward a specific next step (appointment/contract)?

## 4) Final Interview Questions (Acquisition Manager)

1. **Volume**: "How do you manage a pipeline of 50+ active leads without losing speed-to-contact on new ones?"
2. **Objection Handling**: "What's the hardest pricing objection you've ever overcome, and how did you do it?"
3. **Accountability**: "If you miss your appointment target for the week, what's your process for self-correcting by Monday?"
4. **Underwriting**: "Walk me through how you calculate your 'walk-away' price on a deal you really want."
5. **Teamwork**: "How do you handle a handoff from an AI dialer that might have missed a key piece of information?"
