## Role Scorecards and Interview Loops

### AI Dialer Ops Specialist (Week 2)

Scorecard:
- Campaign execution reliability: run scheduled outbound campaigns with less than 2% failed jobs.
- Funnel integrity: maintain webhook/call event completeness above 98%.
- SLA operations: surface and route HOT leads within 5 minutes.
- Continuous optimization: improve connect rate week-over-week through list/time-block tuning.

Interview loop:
1. Recruiter screen (20 min): communication, shift coverage, process discipline.
2. Ops interview (45 min): troubleshooting mindset, dialer operations scenario.
3. Practical exercise (45 min): diagnose a broken outbound campaign from sample logs.
4. Final interview with Acquisition Manager (30 min): ownership and escalation judgment.

### Acquisitions Closer (x2, Weeks 2 and 3)

Scorecard:
- HOT lead speed-to-contact: first human follow-up within 60 minutes.
- Appointment conversion: convert at least 25% of qualified HOT conversations to appointments.
- Offer discipline: maintain MAO guardrails while progressing negotiations.
- CRM hygiene: complete stage updates and next-step notes same day.

Interview loop:
1. Recruiter screen (20 min): compensation fit, schedule fit, high-volume readiness.
2. Sales manager interview (45 min): seller motivation discovery, objection handling.
3. Role-play (45 min): distressed seller negotiation with pricing constraints.
4. Final interview with Acquisition Manager (30 min): forecast accountability and collaboration.

### Disposition Coordinator (Week 4)

Scorecard:
- Buyer activation speed: first buyer outreach within 2 hours of contract-ready package.
- Dispo velocity: median days from market-ready to assignment less than 10 days.
- Margin protection: maintain target assignment fee by structured bid process.
- Buyer relationship quality: repeat-buyer participation above 60%.

Interview loop:
1. Recruiter screen (20 min): communication quality, detail orientation.
2. Ops interview (45 min): buyer list segmentation and blast strategy.
3. Practical exercise (45 min): disposition plan for sample deal packet.
4. Final interview with Acquisition Manager (30 min): execution cadence and handoff quality.

### Part-Time Transaction Coordinator (Week 4)

Scorecard:
- File completeness: contract checklist completion above 98% before close.
- Cycle time: contract-to-close coordination milestones hit on agreed schedule.
- Exception handling: title/escrow blocker escalation same business day.
- Handoff quality: status updates clear for Disposition + Acquisition stakeholders.

Interview loop:
1. Recruiter screen (20 min): transaction operations background, part-time availability.
2. Process interview (45 min): coordination workflow, checklist rigor.
3. Practical exercise (30 min): detect missing items in transaction file and recovery plan.
4. Final interview with Acquisition Manager (20 min): prioritization under parallel files.

## Opening Sequence and Dependencies

1. Open AI Dialer Ops + Closer #1 first (Week 2) to protect HOT lead response and conversation throughput.
2. Open Closer #2 next (Week 3) to sustain sub-1-hour response window while first closer ramps.
3. Open Disposition + part-time Transaction coordination in Week 4 once under-contract pace begins.

Dependency rules:
- Do not delay Closer #2 if Week-2 HOT volume exceeds 8 qualified leads per day.
- Pull Transaction Coordinator opening forward by one week if under-contract backlog exceeds 6 active files.
