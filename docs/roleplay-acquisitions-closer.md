# Practical Exercise: Acquisitions Closer Role-Play Scenario

**Candidate Role**: Acquisitions Closer
**Manager Role**: "Mr./Ms. Thompson," a distressed seller facing foreclosure.

---

## 1. Context (to be given to the candidate)

You are following up on a **HOT lead** from our AI agent. The seller, Mr./Ms. Thompson, is currently behind on payments and has a foreclosure date set for **18 days from now**. Our AI agent already spoke to them and they mentioned they "might be open to an offer but need to talk to a human first."

**Property Details**: 
- **Address**: 123 Oak Lane, DFW.
- **Condition**: Needs major kitchen/bath update, roof leak.
- **Estimated ARV**: $320,000.
- **Our MAO (Max Allowable Offer)**: $185,000.
- **Opening Target Offer**: $165,000.

**Objective**: Empathize with the seller's situation, confirm motivation, and negotiate a contract within the MAO range.

---

## 2. Persona Script (for the interviewer/manager)

**Motivation**: High (needs to move by foreclosure date), but Defensive/Proud.
**Pain Points**: "I've lived here for 20 years. I don't want to leave with nothing."
**Opening Position**: "I've seen houses on Zillow going for $350k. I want at least $250k."

### Seller's "Secret" Responses
- **If the candidate is pushy on price early**: Get defensive. "I'm not a desperate person. I'll just talk to a realtor."
- **If the candidate empathizes first**: Soften. "It's just been a rough year since the layoff. I just need enough to clear the mortgage and have a little for a rental."
- **Mortgage Payoff**: $150,000. (The seller won't volunteer this unless asked if they have a target net amount).
- **Hard Floor**: Needs to walk away with at least **$10,000 cash** to feel "safe." (Meaning anything $160,000+ is technically acceptable, but start at $250k).

---

## 3. Evaluation Checklist for Manager

### [ ] Discovery (Empathy-First)
- Did they ask *why* I need to sell?
- Did they acknowledge the foreclosure timeline without being predatory?
- Did they build rapport before talking about the house condition?

### [ ] Condition Assessment
- Did they ask about the roof, kitchen, and systems to "build the logic" for a lower offer?

### [ ] Price Negotiation
- Did they anchor with a range? (e.g., "Most houses in this condition sell for $160k-$170k")
- Did they justify the offer using repair estimates?
- Did they ask the "Magic Question": "If I can close this in 10 days and pay all your closing costs, what is the absolute best you can do?"

### [ ] Closing / Next Steps
- Did they ask for the signature or a specific appointment to sign?
- Did they explain the Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) and Title process?

### [ ] Ownership & Discipline
- Did they stay below the $185,000 MAO? (Auto-fail if exceeded without escalation).
