## 1) Role Brief (Published)

### Role mission
Secure a detail-obsessed part-time Transaction Coordinator to own the administrative and compliance lifecycle of our real estate deals. This role ensures every contract is complete, every deadline is met, and every closing happens on time.

### Scope
- **File Management**: Ensure 100% completeness of contract checklists before closing.
- **Cycle Time Management**: Track and hit all contract-to-close milestones (inspection periods, earnest money deposits, title commitments).
- **Communication**: Provide clear, daily status updates to Acquisition and Disposition stakeholders.
- **Exception Handling**: Identify and escalate title or escrow blockers on the same business day they arise.
- **Compliance**: Maintain digital deal files in accordance with company standards and state regulations.

### Success metrics (first 30 days)
- **File Completeness**: 100% of contracts checked against checklist within 24 hours of receipt.
- **Cycle Time Compliance**: All milestones hit on schedule or escalated immediately.
- **Exception Resolution**: 95%+ of title/escrow blockers resolved or path-to-resolution defined within 48 hours.
- **Handoff Clarity**: No "missing information" complaints from Dispo or Acquisition teams.

### Required profile
- 2+ years in real estate transaction coordination, escrow officer assistant roles, or real estate administration.
- Proven track record of managing 10+ active files in parallel.
- Extreme attention to detail—never misses a signature or a deadline.
- Proficient with e-signature platforms (DocuSign, HelloSign) and CRMs.
- Professional and clear communication style for title/attorney coordination.

## 3) Practical Exercise: Transaction File Audit

### Scenario
The candidate is given a sample deal folder (under contract).
- **Contract Price**: $200,000.
- **Closing Date**: May 30, 2026.
- **Current Date**: May 15, 2026.
- **Folder Includes**: Executed Sales Contract, Addendum A, and a Title Search.

### Tasks
1. **Gap Analysis**: Identify missing items (e.g., EMD receipt, Seller Disclosure, Lead-Based Paint Disclosure if applicable).
2. **Deadline Map**: Create a timeline for the remaining 15 days, including all critical contingencies.
3. **Escalation Logic**: Draft a message to the seller's agent who hasn't provided the Seller Disclosure for 5 days.
4. **Handoff Check**: Ensure the file is "Dispo Ready" (all buyer/seller info verified).

### 3.1) Ideal Answer Key & Evaluation Rubric

#### 1. Gap Analysis (Critical Items)
*Ideal Answer identifies at minimum:*
- **EMD Receipt**: Must verify the Earnest Money Deposit was actually received by the title company.
- **Seller's Disclosure**: Legal requirement in most states; missing for 5+ days.
- **Lead-Based Paint Disclosure**: Required if the property was built before 1978.
- **Buyer's Entity Docs**: If closing in an LLC, need the Operating Agreement/Articles.

#### 2. Deadline Map (May 15 to May 30)
*Ideal Answer includes:*
- **May 17 (ASAP)**: Follow up on EMD and Seller Disclosure.
- **May 20**: Inspection Period expiry (assuming a 5-day period from May 15).
- **May 22**: Title Commitment review deadline.
- **May 27**: Final Closing Statement (HUD-1/Settlement) draft review.
- **May 29**: Buyer wire confirmation for funds to close.
- **May 30**: RECORDING/CLOSING.

#### 3. Escalation Logic (Draft Message)
*Ideal Answer demonstrates:*
- Professional but firm tone.
- Clear reference to the contract paragraph requiring the disclosure.
- Explicit mention of the potential closing delay if not received.
- Request for a specific "time of delivery" today.

#### 4. Handoff Check (Dispo Ready)
*Ideal Answer confirms:*
- Verified legal names of all parties.
- Assignment of Contract is executed (if applicable).
- Title is clear or the specific "cloud" is identified with a path to cure.
